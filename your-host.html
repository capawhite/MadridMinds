Meet Your Host

Ruy

Host of Madrid Minds — a weekly English-speaking chess social night for meeting interesting people, and collaborative team experiences for companies in Madrid.

I created Madrid Minds because some things are just better shared.

I've spent over 20 years in the chess industry, helping build an online platform that made the game more accessible, engaging, and genuinely fun for people around the world. Chess has a rare gift: it brings interesting people together and gives them a reason to talk — the kind of evenings where you meet people worth knowing.

— Ruy
