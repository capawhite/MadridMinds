Meet Interesting People Over Chess

A weekly English-speaking chess social night in Madrid, built around meeting new people. The evening opens with a short, story-led chess talk, then flows into casual games and easy conversation. Chess is the spark — the person you hadn't met yet is what stays with you.

Beginning September 2026 · Currently every Tuesday at 8PM · Abya Restaurante, Calle Ortega y Gasset 32, Madrid.

  • A short, story-led chess talk — nothing technical, no experience needed
  • Casual games and open conversation — expats, locals & newcomers welcome
  • Spanish wine & tapas set the mood; non-alcoholic options always available
  • Weekly · Tuesdays 8PM · All in English
