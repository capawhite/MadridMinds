An English-speaking chess social

Madrid has a lively chess scene, but most of it runs in Spanish and assumes you already play. Madrid Minds is different: it's an English-speaking evening built as much for meeting people as for the game itself. The night opens with a short, story-led chess talk, then flows into casual games and open conversation.

Chess is the spark, not a test. Come alone or with friends — you'll leave having met someone new.

Who it's for

  • Expats and internationals who want to play and meet people in English
  • Complete beginners — no experience or rating needed
  • Returning players looking for a relaxed, social game
  • Anyone new to Madrid who wants a regular, easy way to meet people

What to expect

  • A short, story-led chess talk — nothing technical
  • Casual games at every level, and people happy to teach
  • Spanish wine and tapas to set the mood, with non-alcoholic options
  • All in English · Weekly · Tuesdays at 8PM
