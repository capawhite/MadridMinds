Common questions

Do we need to know chess?
No. Challenges are designed for people who have never played. Prior chess knowledge is not an advantage.
Is alcohol required?
No. Many teams book alcohol-free formats. Wine and tapas are an optional add-on, usually after the session.
Is it in English?
Yes. Madrid Minds for Teams is delivered in English — ideal for international and expat teams in Madrid.
Is this an HR training course?
No. It is a team experience — a structured, facilitated session with optional debrief, not accredited classroom training.
