# Madrid Minds — Direction Brief

Context handoff for working on the site in an editor (Cursor / Claude Code).
This captures positioning decisions so you don't have to re-explain them.

---

## What Madrid Minds is (current positioning)

- **English-language by design.** Audience = English-speaking internationals /
  expats in Madrid who want to meet people. Not Spanish-first. This is the edge,
  not a limitation — it's also the least-served slice of "chess in Madrid" search.
- **Chess is the vehicle, social connection is the outcome.** Lead with chess as
  the distinctive *mechanism* ("meet interesting people over chess"); sell the
  friendships/connection as the *benefit*. Do NOT dilute into a generic expat
  mixer — chess is the wedge that makes it searchable and un-generic.
- **Two offerings, different jobs:**
  - **Weekly social chess night** = brand + community + content + top-of-funnel.
    Format: a short chess lecture + casual games, at a premium venue, once a week.
    Small direct revenue; its real job is credibility + leads.
  - **Corporate "team strategy experiences"** = the revenue engine. Higher ticket,
    faster cash. The social nights are the live proof-of-concept shown to corporate
    buyers.
- **Name stays "Madrid Minds."** It's deliberately broader than chess, which
  supports the social-first direction and leaves room to add other "minds"
  formats later. No rebrand.

## Decisions that change the current site

- **Drop the structured wine-tasting pillar.** Keep drinks as ambiance, but stop
  positioning "wine tasting" as a produced feature. Simplify the concept.
- **Add / sharpen a "where to play chess in Madrid" angle** as an SEO + landing
  hook (English). Someone searching that should land on: "there's a weekly
  English-speaking chess social at [venue], [day]." This is now a real funnel.
- **Make the corporate arm a first-class part of the site**, not a footnote —
  it's likely the primary revenue.

## Site review — what to look at and fix

1. **Homepage hero / above the fold**
   - One clear line: chess-led social evenings for English-speakers in Madrid.
   - Primary CTA: join the next social night (or waitlist/newsletter).
   - Secondary CTA: corporate team experiences → dedicated page.

2. **Copy audit for the chess-as-vehicle / social-as-outcome framing**
   - Flag copy that reads as generic "social gathering" with no hook.
   - Flag copy still built around wine tasting.
   - Ensure "All in English" stays visible — it's a selling point.

3. **"Play chess in Madrid" landing content (SEO)**
   - A page targeting English chess-in-Madrid intent that routes to the weekly night.
   - Consider a light "guide to chess in Madrid" for organic reach that funnels in.

4. **Corporate page**
   - What a "team strategy experience" includes, who it's for, how to book/enquire.
   - Simple enquiry path (form → email).

5. **Newsletter / lead capture**
   - Soft capture on every page (most visitors won't buy today but will subscribe).

---

## Priority order

1. Homepage hero + core positioning copy (chess-led, English, social outcome).
2. Corporate page (revenue engine).
3. "Chess in Madrid" SEO landing + funnel to weekly night.
4. Remove/soften wine-tasting framing throughout.
5. Newsletter capture everywhere.

## Open questions still to resolve (not blockers for the site)

- Willingness to do corporate B2B outreach (that's where income-soon lives).
- Venue + fixed weekly day for the social night.
- Pricing for both the night and corporate packages.
