Who this is for

  • You already know how the pieces move
  • You’ve played a game or two and want more than the rules
  • You finished The First Move and want the next evening

If you’ve never played, start with The First Move. That’s the unintimidating way in.

What happens

  • Simple tactics you can actually use
  • Threats, captures and piece values
  • Pattern recognition through play
  • Development and making stronger decisions

What you leave knowing

How to notice when something is hanging. How to look one idea further than the obvious move. Enough pattern language to feel the board start to speak — without drowning in theory.
