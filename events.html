The weekly chess night

Our English-speaking chess social night runs every week in Madrid — for expats, locals and newcomers who want to meet interesting people over chess. A short chess talk, casual games, and easy conversation. No experience needed.

English chess social night

A short, story-led chess talk to open the evening, then casual games and open conversation over wine and tapas. Come alone or with friends — beginners always welcome.

Tuesdays · 8PM · Abya Restaurante, Calle Ortega y Gasset 32, Madrid · English

What to expect

  • A short, story-led chess talk — nothing technical
  • Casual games at every level — no experience needed
  • Conversation with curious, interesting people
  • Spanish wine and tapas to set the mood; non-alcoholic options too
