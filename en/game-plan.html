Who this is for

  • You can play a full game without getting lost in the rules
  • Your openings feel random and you want a better start
  • You’ve done Think Ahead, or something like it

New to chess? The First Move is the right first evening.

What happens

  • How to start a game confidently
  • Opening principles: development, the centre, king safety
  • Basic plans and common mistakes
  • How to leave the opening with something to do

What you leave knowing

A handful of principles you can reuse every time you sit down. A way to start, develop, and keep the king safe — then transition into a meaningful plan.
