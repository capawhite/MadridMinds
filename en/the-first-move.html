The First Move

Your first chess evening.

Chess is easier than you think. A two-hour experience for complete beginners — fun, surprising, and unintimidating. Not Lesson 1 of a course.

Never played? Perfect.

Who this is for

  • You have never played
  • Someone taught you years ago and you forgot
  • You vaguely know how the pieces move
  • Chess has always looked interesting but intimidating

If you already play regularly, this evening isn’t designed for you — write to us and we’ll point you to a better fit.

What happens

  • Warm-up games that make the board feel friendly
  • The essentials, taught through patterns rather than lectures
  • Stories and surprises that give chess a human texture
  • Guided play, then a complete game of your own

What you leave knowing

How the pieces move. What the game is actually asking of you. And the feeling of having played a real game — not just watched one. That is the whole point of the evening.

By the end of the evening, you’ll have played a real game.
