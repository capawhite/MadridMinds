What the experience looks like

  1. Learn

    Start with the essentials through fun, accessible activities. Whether someone is a complete beginner or already knows the game, everyone can take part.

  2. Think

    Explore intriguing chess challenges together. Share ideas, compare approaches, and discover how differently people can see the same situation.

  3. Play

    Put everything into practice with guided games, friendly competition, and plenty of opportunities to laugh, talk, and connect.

Why chess works for teams

  • Connect beyond work

    Bring colleagues from different roles around the same board. Job titles matter less, conversations come naturally, and friendly competition helps people connect in unexpected ways.

  • Think differently

    Chess encourages curiosity, creative thinking, and looking at challenges from different perspectives. Every position offers an opportunity to explore options, make decisions, and adapt.

  • Discover and learn together

    Everyone starts somewhere. Chess makes learning tangible: experiment, make mistakes, try new ideas, and enjoy the satisfaction of discovering something new.

  • Enjoy a real break

    Step away from screens, meetings, and everyday routines. Share an engaging activity that combines mental stimulation, social interaction, and the simple pleasure of playing together.
