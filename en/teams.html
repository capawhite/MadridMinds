Common questions

Do people need to know chess?
No. The experience is designed so previous chess knowledge is not an advantage.
What language do you use?
English, Spanish, or bilingual — whichever fits the team. Delivery options for Spanish companies and international groups alike.
How is language handled?
The session runs in the language you choose. People talk through puzzles and decisions together — the product is the team experience.
Where does it happen?
Your venue, or a private room in Madrid. Group size is flexible; tell us the number when you write.
What kind of session is this?
A facilitated team experience. Useful things surface in the room; it is built around play, not slides.
