Think together. Play together.

A chess evening for teams — beginners welcome. Practice decisions together, enjoy a real break from the usual meeting, and share the table over wine and snacks.

What the experience looks like

  1. Learn

    Start with the essentials so everyone can take part.

  2. Think

    Work through positions, ideas and decisions together.

  3. Play

    Finish with guided games and friendly competition.

What teams get from the evening

  • Decisions

    Every position asks people to compare options and choose — with limited time and real consequences on the board.

  • Planning

    Players think ahead, weigh short-term moves against longer goals, and adjust when the position changes.

  • Learning

    Mistakes are visible and useful. Reviewing a game is a natural way to talk about feedback without a lecture.

  • Connection

    Colleagues from different roles share one board — conversation, friendly competition, and a reason to talk beyond work.

A real evening together — not a training workshop.
