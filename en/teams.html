MadridMinds for Teams

A team experience that puts focus, strategy and decision-making into play.

Chess gives people something real to think about together: recognising patterns, weighing options, planning ahead and making decisions. MadridMinds turns that into an engaging team experience that complete beginners can enjoy.

Designed so complete beginners can take part from the start.

Request a proposal

Focus

Chess asks people to slow down, pay attention and stay engaged with the problem in front of them.

Decision-making

Every position creates choices. Teams weigh options, discuss ideas and decide what to do next.

Strategic thinking

Participants learn to look beyond the immediate move, recognise patterns and think ahead.

Shared challenge

Everyone works on something new together, creating conversation, interaction and friendly competition.

What the experience brings together

MadridMinds combines active learning, mental challenge and social interaction in one experience. During the session, people use concentration, pattern recognition, planning, problem-solving and friendly competition — inside a shared game.

The MadridMinds Team Experience

About 90–120 minutes. Flexible group size and venue — at your office, a private room, or a space we help arrange.
