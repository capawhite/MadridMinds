About

Chess shouldn’t feel intimidating.

It has acquired a reputation for being complicated, serious and reserved for people who were already good. MadridMinds was created around the opposite idea.

Why MadridMinds exists

Most adults who are curious about chess never start. The clubs feel too serious. The internet feels too loud. A friend once showed them the pieces, once, years ago.

MadridMinds is built around standalone chess experiences for adults. Premium without being pretentious. Social without being a mixer. Experiences are hosted in English — because that is who they are for, and because Madrid already has plenty of Spanish-speaking chess.

Chess should feel approachable before it feels impressive. Not a game for geniuses. A game for curious people.

There’s something quietly valuable about spending an evening thinking, focusing and sitting across from another person instead of consuming another screen.

The evenings

Designed so a complete beginner can sit down, enjoy the room, and leave having actually played.
