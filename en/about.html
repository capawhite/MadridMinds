Why MadridMinds

Chess is one of the world’s great games: strategic, social and endlessly interesting. MadridMinds makes it accessible through evenings where people can learn, play and enjoy the company around them.

Every game asks you to focus, recognise patterns, weigh options, plan ahead and make decisions — with other people at the table. The challenge is real; the evening is meant to be enjoyed.

Evenings are in English — a natural fit for Madrid’s international crowd.

A different kind of evening.

Small groups, a comfortable setting, wine and snacks, good conversation and time to actually engage with the people around you. Come to learn, come with a friend, or simply come because you want to try something different.
