About MadridMinds

More than learning chess.

MadridMinds brings together chess, conversation and a relaxed setting for adults who want to learn something new and spend an evening doing something genuinely engaging.

Why MadridMinds

Chess is one of the world’s great games: strategic, social and endlessly interesting. MadridMinds makes it accessible through carefully designed experiences where people can learn, play and enjoy the company around them.

Every game asks you to focus, recognise patterns, weigh options, plan ahead and make decisions. The challenge is real, but the experience is designed to make it enjoyable.

Experiences are hosted in English, giving MadridMinds a distinctive place in Madrid’s international and professional community.

A different kind of evening.

Small groups, a comfortable setting, good conversation and time to actually engage with the people around you. Come to learn, come with a friend, or simply come because you want to try something different.
