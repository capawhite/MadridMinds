About

Chess shouldn’t feel intimidating.

It has acquired a reputation for being complicated, serious and reserved for people who were already good. MadridMinds was created around the opposite idea.

The idea

Most adults who are curious about chess never start. The clubs feel too serious. The internet feels too loud. A friend once showed them the pieces, once, years ago.

MadridMinds is a boutique evening in Madrid where that curiosity is enough. Premium without being pretentious. Social without being a mixer. In English, because that is who it is for — and because the city already has plenty of Spanish-speaking chess. It is not an English class. It is not a school.

The philosophy is simple: not a game for geniuses. A game for curious people.

There is also something quietly valuable about an evening spent actively thinking, focusing and sitting across from another person — rather than consuming another screen. That is a supporting reason to come. It is not a medical claim, and it is not the brand.

The evenings

Designed so a complete beginner can sit down, enjoy the room, and leave having actually played.

The person in the room is part of that atmosphere — not a lecture, not a performance. Name, portrait and bio will live here when they are ready. For now the offer is the idea: chess shouldn’t feel intimidating.

Think chess isn’t for you?

We’d like two hours to change your mind.
